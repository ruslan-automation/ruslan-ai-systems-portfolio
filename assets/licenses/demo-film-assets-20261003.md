# Four demo films, October 3, 2026

The published videos are staged product demonstrations, not recordings of real
customer conversations or actual appointments. Homepage teasers have no audio
stream. Full films start muted; sound is available through the player.

## Music

- Knowledge base and kitchen consultant: Hazy After Hours by Alejandro Magana,
  Mixkit track 132: https://assets.mixkit.co/music/132/132.mp3.
- Kitchen questionnaire and voice administrator: Stylz by Ahjay Stelino,
  Mixkit track 102: https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/download/102/.
- These tracks are included only as edited audiovisual soundtracks, under the
  Mixkit Stock Music Free License: https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree.
- The accompanying license snapshot is `mixkit-music-license.txt`. Music is not
  offered as a standalone download or claimed as an original composition.

## Voice administrator

Both published full formats contain music and sound effects only. They contain
no synthetic dialogue generated on the ElevenLabs Free plan. The portrait
version uses the same music-only audio stream as the landscape version.

ElevenLabs Free output is excluded from this promotional website because that
plan does not include commercial usage rights. Source:
https://help.elevenlabs.io/hc/en-us/articles/13313564601361-Can-I-publish-the-content-I-generate-on-the-platform.

## Visual material

Kitchen and clinic photos were generated for the films. Motion design and 3D
scenes were authored for these demonstrations. Posters are frames from the
same approved video exports. Fonts and interface icons are rasterized into
the video; font files and the animation runtime are not distributed here.
