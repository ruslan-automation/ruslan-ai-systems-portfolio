# Kitchen Questionnaire Film v2

Approved review exports dated 2026-10-03, copied without transcoding:

- `media/questionnaire-film-landscape-v2.mp4`: 1920 x 1080, 60 fps, 22 seconds.
- `media/questionnaire-film-portrait-v2.mp4`: 1080 x 1920, 60 fps, 22 seconds.
- `media/questionnaire-teaser-v2.mp4`: 960 x 600, 30 fps, 9 seconds, no audio.

Source project: `output/portfolio-questionnaire-film-20261003/v2`.

Kitchen image generated for this project with the built-in image-generation
tool. The 3D scene and animation are locally authored with Three.js and GSAP.
Manrope and Unbounded fonts and Lucide icons are rendered into the video.
WebP posters are stills from the approved videos at 1.5 seconds.

Music: Stylz by Ahjay Stelino, Mixkit Stock Music Free License.

- Catalog: https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/electronic/
- Official download: https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/download/102/?context=item+grid
- License: https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree
- License snapshot: `mixkit-music-license.txt` in this directory.

The full film contains a 22-second music edit with a gentle opening, filtered
upper frequencies and a 1.6-second ending fade. Transition sounds were
synthesized for the local film project. No assets from the direction-reference
films were used in these exports.
